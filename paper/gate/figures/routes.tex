\Description{Three language boxes, C, RISC-V, and BTOR2, with the two
routes from C to a search at BTOR2 drawn above, and three rows below
showing where a witness, a certificate, and a bare claim are judged
on the way back: the witness at C, the certificate at BTOR2 and then
at C, the bare claim nowhere.}
\caption{One question, two routes (\cref{sec:overview}). Above: the
question lives at C; route one crosses one pair to the searches at
BTOR2, route two crosses two. Below: what a search writes travels
back and is judged where it lands --- a filled dot is an interpreter
or checker run, and the gap counts the hops between the question and
the last one. A witness is always replayed at home. A certificate is
discharged where it lands --- at gap 1 its residual is the lineage of
\code{c--btor2} plus BTOR2's judge --- and, once the pair carries
certificates, again at home by \code{regrade}. A bare claim is judged
nowhere.}
