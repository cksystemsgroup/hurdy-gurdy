\section*{Data Availability Statement}

The system is one repository, and the paper describes one frozen
commit of it: the kernel and its tests, the second lineage of its
pure half, the Lean development with its axiom audit, every admitted
entry of the registry with the evidence of its admission, the oracle
packs with the provenance of every expected value, among them the
competitions' published result tables and the host calibration of
their solvers, the four pinned
benchmarks with their append-only logs, and the fault-injection
deposit. Every number in the paper is read from that commit: the
boards regenerate byte for byte from the logs, the fault-injection
tables from the deposit, and the trusted-base table from the
registry, each by one command of the kernel's driver, and a table in
the repository names the file each number came from. The repository
is public; its address is withheld here for anonymity and will be
given in the final version. The artifact will be submitted for artifact evaluation; the outside
tools that confirmed the oracle packs' expected values
(\cref{sec:closing}) are not part of it.
