\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Generated verification infrastructure}
Language models are used to write proofs and repair them until a
checker accepts them
\citep{first2023baldur,yang2023leandojo,yang2025autoverus}, to
propose invariants for a verifier to confirm
\citep{pei2023invariants,wu2024lemur}, to synthesize code together
with its specification and proof
\citep{misu2024dafny,aggarwal2025alphaverus}, and to keep code,
documentation, and tests consistent under a checker's eye
\citep{sun2024clover}. In every case
the checker is fixed and trusted and the model fills one slot; here
the model fills every slot and trust moves to a property no slot can
assert, where along a route an interpreter run judged the evidence. \citet{hurdygurdy2026preprint} gives a calculus
of directional squares, a componentwise meet for composed
translations, and the asymmetry between replayed witnesses and
universal claims, for a platform of hand-wrapped engines; the design
here consumes that calculus and nothing else of that platform.

\paragraph{Translation validation and verified compilation}
Checking a translation per program rather than proving the translator
\citep{pnueli1998tv,necula2000tv} is the square of \cref{sec:pairs},
with two differences: both sides are generated interpreters rather
than a trusted semantics, and the check is on a shipped corpus at
admission with mutants that must fail, not on every compilation.
Verified compilers \citep{leroy2009compcert,kumar2014cakeml} and
verified translation validators \citep{tristan2008tv,sewell2013tv}
buy a stronger statement at the cost of a proof per translator;
Alive2 \citep{lopes2021alive2} bounds the check as a bound cap bounds
a claim here. The system proves no translator; what it adds is
that a result's grade never depends on whether a translator was
right, only on whether a judge ran.

\paragraph{Certificates, witnesses, and proof formats}
Certifying model checkers \citep{namjoshi2001certifying}, certified
hardware model checking \citep{yu2021certifaiger}, verification
witnesses \citep{beyer2015witnesses,beyer2022witnesses},
proof-carrying code \citep{necula1997pcc}, certifying algorithms
\citep{mcconnell2011certifying}, and checked proof formats
\citep{wetzler2014drat,tan2021cakelpr,andreotti2023carcara} share the
move that an untrusted producer emits an object a small checker
validates. Here the checker is generated too, admitted by its own
controls, and the object's grade records how many translations lie
between the checker that validated it and the question. Certified
abstract interpretation \citep{besson2006pcc,jourdan2015verasco}
produces or verifies the kind of certificate \code{c:ranges} checks;
the difference is that the analysis here is untrusted and the checker
is what was gated.

\paragraph{The searches}
The generated searches implement known algorithms: bounded model
checking \citep{biere1999bmc}, k-induction
\citep{sheeran2000kinduction}, IC3 and property-directed reachability
\citep{bradley2011ic3,een2011pdr}, interval abstract interpretation
\citep{cousot1977abstract}; \cref{tab:oracle} records their distance
from the competitions' solvers. Btor2C
\citep{beyer2023btor2c} translates BTOR2 machines to C so that
software verifiers reach hardware questions; the reverse edge is the
same move gated in both directions, a negative at the tag.

\paragraph{Mutation analysis}
Mutation testing measures a test suite by the faults it detects
\citep{demillo1978hints,jia2011mutation}. \Cref{sec:faults} turns it
on a verification system in three ways: the ``test suite'' a mutant faces is the admission gate; a mutant of an untrusted component can be executed past the
gate and its outputs scored by the grade the system itself assigns;
and a surviving mutant of a checker is classified by direction, since
only one direction is unsound.

\paragraph{The trusted base}
That trust cannot be bootstrapped from a component one did not judge
is old \citep{thompson1984trust}; the LCF discipline of a small
kernel every proof must pass \citep{gordon1979lcf} is the shape of
the trusted base here, the kernel's own standing established by
proof, tests, a second lineage, and pins, not by being small. Differential testing
\citep{mckeeman1998difftest,yang2011csmith} and N-version programming
\citep{avizienis1985nversion} supply the second-lineage discipline;
independently written versions fail together
\citep{knight1986independence}, so the corroborated flag requires
declared disjoint descent. Rotor \citep{rotor2017basics} fixes one
translation from RISC-V to BTOR2 by hand; the pairs through RISC-V
are translations of that kind generated and gated, the ISA semantics
generated too, with Sail's model \citep{armstrong2019sail} as an
oracle for the interpreter's vectors.
