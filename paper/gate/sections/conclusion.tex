\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The limits are the ones the tree states. The square is empirical: a
pair is admitted when its squares close on its corpus, and a corpus
program is not every program; the mutants make the corpus
falsifiable, not complete. The seal is a blank \code{PATH} and an
emptied environment, measured by tests; an absolute path to a tool is
hidden by nothing but the committed source that would show it. The
corroborated flag has never fired, because every route shares the C
front end and one generator, and the second BTOR2 lineage the fifth
generation had --- a BDD search written from the format's semantics
alone --- is not regenerated. The judges are not all small: BTOR2's
are 390, 1,618, and 1,620 lines, but C's \code{induction} and
\code{ranges} checkers are 4,534 and 3,935 lines, because each embeds
the front end, the RISC-V-road encoding, and the bit-blaster it
judges with; the honesty metric of \cref{sec:evidence} says so rather
than hiding it. The reach table, the oracle column, and the
per-domain ledger are specified and not drawn; no domain records
performance testimony, so the boards stand beside the archives'
verdicts and not their times. And the fifth generation's boards on
the same three hardware benchmarks --- 46 of 74, 5 of 55, 45 of 80,
under a trust story in which searches pronounced verdicts on their
own admitted word and with a BDD search this generation has not
regenerated --- were higher on two of the three; that is what
removing the seam and refusing arrays at three of five searches cost,
recorded in the history and not rounded away.

Within those limits the claim is exact in kind. A verification system
can be built entirely from generated, untrusted text ---
interpreters, translators, searches, checkers, carry-backs, and the
kernel itself --- and judged whole: one gate admits everything by
running it against its own controls; one trust event, an interpreter
run, is the only source of truth; a result's grade is the distance
from the question to the last such run, and its residual is computed
by removing everything upstream of that run; and the kernel that
does this is proved where it is mathematics, falsified where it is
operation, doubled by a lineage that shares nothing with it, and
pinned. At one frozen commit that system holds four boards whose
every judged answer agrees with the competitions' verdicts, 32
universal answers that rest on a home judge alone, and three honest
negatives --- an edge that returned only partials, a rung refused by
three of five searches, and ten minutes that bought one question ---
reported as results, because the design leaves no other way to report
them. The generator never writes a result; only the kernel does, by
running judges over transported evidence. What moves the boards next
is a search that reasons differently, admitted through the same gate,
and the boards will say whether it did.
