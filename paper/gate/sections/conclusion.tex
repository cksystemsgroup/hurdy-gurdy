\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The limits are the ones the tree states. The square is empirical: a
pair is admitted when its squares close on its corpus, and a corpus
program is not every program; the mutants make the corpus
falsifiable, not complete. The judges are not small ---
C's two checkers are 4,534 and 3,935 lines --- and after every
revision (\cref{sec:faults}) 247
of 700 sampled judge mutants still pass admission, and for the 217
checker mutants among them the evidence that they are harmless is a
pool of certificates, not a proof. The corroborated flag has fired
between two searches of disjoint descent and never between two
judges: every answer still rests on one lineage of interpreter and
checker per language. Candidates the gate refused left no trace, so the
generator's failure rate is not measured. The per-domain ledger is
specified and not drawn; the oracle column reads that the generated
searches are weak beside the competitions' solvers: no board holds a
question they left unsolved.

Within those limits, a verification system
can be built entirely from generated, untrusted text and judged
whole: one gate admits everything against its own controls, one trust
event --- an interpreter run --- is the only source of truth, and a
result's grade is the distance from the question to the last such
run. The claim that follows from the design --- a
faulty transport can cost an answer and cannot forge one --- held on
every one of 16,720 plays of faulty transports with the gate removed.
The claim that does not follow from it --- that the judges deserve
their place --- failed in six places, each found by the system's own
instruments and closed by tests whose answers came from outside. What
the frontier asks for next is smaller judges, a second lineage of
them, and the bottlenecks \cref{sec:performance} names. A domain of
mathematics, physics, chemistry, or biology enters as hardware and
software did --- its root's interpreter and one pair to a hub,
or a new hub with that domain's solvers as searches, trusted no more
than the generated ones --- and from then on every search reaches it
and a question carried in is reasoned about across domains, at a
cost not measured here.
