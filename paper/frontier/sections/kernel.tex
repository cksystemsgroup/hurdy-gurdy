\section{Languages, pairs, results}
\label{sec:kernel}

\paragraph{Languages}
A \emph{language} is a deterministic syntax plus a deterministic
interpreter exposing named \emph{observables}. Two kinds. \emph{Root}
languages --- the formats benchmarks arrive in --- are the trusted
base: their interpreters are graded \emph{stipulated} until
corroborated by benchmark labels or supplied test vectors. Only roots
cost trust. A \emph{derived} language is always registered together
with a pair to a parent --- an \emph{abstraction}
(over-approximating) or a \emph{specialization} (a fragment with an
exact embedding) --- so its semantics is checked against the
parent's and it adds nothing to the trusted base.

\paragraph{Pairs}
The unit of the platform is the \emph{pair}: a directed edge with a
pure translator $T$, a carry-back map $\Lambda$, kept observables
$\pi$, a direction (exact / over / under), measured cost, and a
lineage declaration. There are two kinds of pair and \emph{one} gate
(\cref{fig:edges}):

\begin{itemize}
\item A \emph{translation pair} (language $\to$ language) is checked
  per program by the directional commuting square,
  $I_s(p) \equiv_\pi \Lambda(I_t(T(p)))$: interpreting the source
  agrees, on $\pi$, with translating, interpreting the translation,
  and carrying the behavior back. An abstraction pair declares
  $\sqsubseteq_\pi$ instead --- every source behavior has a target
  counterpart, and the target may deliberately have more ---
  universal verdicts transfer back across such a hop, and
  existential ones only ever return by replay. The square is
  decidable per program: run both sides, compare under $\pi$; a
  failure names its step and observable.
\item A \emph{solver pair} (language $\to$ \emph{results}) has the
  result language as its target: $T$ \emph{is} solving under a
  declared budget. The square degenerates to \emph{result validity}
  --- witnesses must replay at the source, certificates must
  re-discharge, supplied negative controls must fail, determinism
  must hold --- under the same admission discipline. There is no
  separate solver gate.
\end{itemize}

Pairs compose into \emph{routes}, and every playable route ends in a
solver pair. A route's contract is the componentwise meet of its
hops' contracts --- the weakest hop on every axis at once --- the
one composition rule kept from the previous
calculus~\citep{hurdygurdy2026calculus}: a route's label never
overstates.

\paragraph{Results}
A result is a fixed kernel schema with domain-specific payloads:
\[
\begin{array}{r@{\ }l}
\textit{result} = & \{\, q,\ \textit{route},\ \textit{budget},\
  \textit{grade},\ \textit{value} \,\} \\[2pt]
\textit{value} = & \textit{witness}(w, \textit{depth}) \\
\mid & \textit{all}(\textit{bound}: k \,|\, \infty,\ \textit{cert}?) \\
\mid & \textit{partial}(\textit{evidence})
\end{array}
\]
$\textit{witness}$ says \emph{here is a $w$} --- and a recorded
witness is always \emph{replayed} (\cref{sec:cert}); a solver's
unreplayable \textit{sat} is never a witness result, only evidence
inside a partial. $\textit{all}$ says \emph{none exists up to the
bound} (or unboundedly, certificate permitting). $\textit{partial}$
is deliberately semi-structured: a small typed core the kernel
orders on --- bound reached, budget spent --- plus free-form
progress description and solver profiling, because its reader is the
conjecturing LLM, not a derivation engine. This is the rule that
keeps the redesign's collapse honest: \textbf{structure only what
the kernel must compute with; everything else is evidence and stays
prose.} Budgets ride in every result's provenance; capped is labeled
capped; cost is recorded and reported, never ranked.

\paragraph{Determinism, measured}
Every registered executable --- interpreter, translator, solver
shim --- is a pure deterministic function from bytes to bytes, run
sandboxed (own process, empty environment, temporary working
directory, wall and memory caps). The kernel does not believe this;
it measures it: \emph{every check runs twice and byte-compares the
output}. Determinism is the load-bearing wall --- it is what makes
the square decidable per program, replay evidence rather than
anecdote, and every map entry re-derivable.
