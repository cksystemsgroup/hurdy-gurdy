\section{Certification and the grade ladder}
\label{sec:cert}

Both certifications are carry-back-then-check --- $\Lambda$, then a
run --- and they are mirrors (\cref{fig:ladder}); the difference is
what checks.

\paragraph{Existential}
A witness is carried back through each hop's $\Lambda$ and
\emph{replayed} through the source interpreter: one run, always
available, route-independent. A wrong or adversarial pair cannot
forge it --- the interpreter that decides is the trusted base, not
anything the route's author wrote
(\textsf{existential\_self\_certifying} in the companion
calculus~\citep{hurdygurdy2026calculus}, axiom-free). On an
abstraction route a witness that fails replay is a \emph{spurious
counterexample}: not a bug but a refinement demand, recorded inside
a partial.

\paragraph{Universal}
A certificate is interpreted \emph{against the problem} --- the
checker is a function of the problem and the certificate, never of
the solver's say-so. Checked at the \emph{target}, the claim
inherits the route's trust (the meet). Carried back and
\emph{re-discharged at the source} --- finitely many decidable
obligations; for an inductive invariant: base, step, safe --- it
becomes route-independent, the exact mirror of witness replay. The
fail-safe direction is definitional: a wrong or wrongly-carried
certificate can only fail to upgrade a claim, never fake one.

\caption{The strict grade ladder. Universal claims climb
\textit{claimed} $<$ \textit{checked} $<$ \textit{certified};
\emph{certified} is reserved for the route-independent form, and
every result states its rung and what its residual trust rests on

\textit{certified}. Existential answers do not climb: a recorded
witness is always \emph{replayed}, which is the same
carry-back-then-check evidence as re-discharge and shares its rung.
\textit{Corroborated} --- agreement across disjoint declared
lineages --- is orthogonal to the ladder.}

The kernel computes grades from lineage declarations, and grades
only improve (\cref{sec:proofs}). Nothing is ever worded stronger
than what was verified. One intended second-order effect: whatever
earns the top grade is what the autonomous loop learns to build, so
the frontier evidence itself pushes the LLM toward certificate
printers, carry-back maps for certificates, and independent
discharge engines.

\paragraph{Contradictions}
A replayed witness is ground truth. If a question ever holds both a
replayed witness and a covering universal claim, the kernel records
a \emph{contradiction event}: the witness stands, the universal's
certification chain is marked falsified, and nothing is silently
resolved. Contradiction events are the platform's fire alarm --- the
one record that says a graded chain lied --- and their permanence is
what makes the grades meaningful.
