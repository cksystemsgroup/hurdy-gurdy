\section{Where the experiments stand}
\label{sec:status}

The protocol of \cref{sec:kit-protocol} has run its first campaign:
seven loop iterations over the pinned suite
\texttt{hwmcc-sosylab-beem} --- 110 reachability questions from the
community HWMCC mirror at a fixed commit, every instance
sha256-pinned, five carrying external labels. This section is
deliberately a summary, not the report: every number below is read
from the deposited ledger, the report regenerates from that ledger
byte-identically, and the caps of every iteration ride in every
record's provenance. Across all seven iterations, every verdict
agreed with every standing label, and no disagreement event was ever
booked.

\paragraph{The arc, in four movements}
The native hub alone answers 79 of 110; the 31 blocked questions
cluster onto a \emph{single} in-set frontier entry, and the ascending
probe measures their cost curves (it.~0). The promoted abstraction
plays and is spent --- 27 of 31 reach the CEGAR cap with every
counterexample spurious, localization has no free room --- so the
flat curve is a finding, and the board entry advances to
\emph{native-procedure}: the books demand the unbounded engine before
anyone builds it, with exponential-in-$k$ fits predicting that deeper
bounded search cannot close the cluster (its.~1--2). The demanded
engine enters through its solver brief and gate and confirms the
prediction: \texttt{pono}'s $k$-induction proves one pin unreachable
at every depth, and tripling the unbounded attention (three modes,
doubled walls, ${\sim}15$\,h of declared spend) then closes nothing
new --- the strongest saturation evidence the books had held
(its.~3--4). Finally the enumeration: every family member never yet
played, run against every blocked pin. Iteration 5 (seven members,
${\sim}34$\,h) closes zero blocked pins but catches two at the wall's
edge, including the campaign's second at-every-depth proof; iteration
6 plays the last two members --- and the last member moves the
frontier. \textsc{abc}'s PDR proves two pins unreachable at every
depth in under twelve minutes each, pins that had resisted every
other engine the platform holds across six iterations. Their books
read like a controlled experiment: blocked by bounded search, by the
abstraction, by \texttt{pono}'s unbounded modes at two walls, by the
seven exploration members, by \texttt{msat-ic3ia} in the same
iteration --- then closed by the bit-level IC3/PDR reference
implementation.

\paragraph{The bottom line}
82 of 110 questions have been answered at least once --- the
quantity \cref{thm:monotone} makes monotone, and it never receded;
the per-iteration column above prices each iteration's own portfolio,
which may honestly dip. Four answers hold at every depth. The
terminal board holds one entry, \texttt{d4c59dafc402} [in-set]
\emph{native-procedure}, down to 29 citing questions, every one
\textit{origin=campaign} --- manufactured demand displayed apart from
organic, by construction. The exit code says \emph{not saturated}:
an in-set entry blocks saturation honestly (\cref{def:saturation}).
What \emph{is} finished is the host's enumeration: BMC, $k$-induction,
every registered IC3 variant, interpolation, synthesis-guided PDR,
and the havoc reduction have all been played and spent against the
blocked cluster. The deposit is the measured practice-frontier of
this host's known set, and the board entry is exportable as a
challenge bundle. Trust is priced, not laundered: every
\textit{reachable} carries a witness that replays through the shared
interpreter; bounded \textit{unreachable}s are priced as bounded
claims; the two \texttt{pono}-family closures spent their certificate
obligation and booked a \emph{typed} no-certificate event (no
generator in the declared pool printed an invariant within its wall
--- a measured gap of the campaign, not the tools), the two
\textsc{abc} closures' obligations stand open, and no unbounded
closure has yet earned cross-lineage corroboration.

\paragraph{Beside the campaign}
The player itself is measured in the instrument
paper~\citep{hurdygurdy2026calculus}: over the platform's MCP
surface, with tool discovery part of the task, an unaided LLM
answered 7 of 8 calibrated reachability questions and the same model
playing the platform answered 8 of 8 --- and the difference is what
the verdict rests on, since every platform answer carries the
artifact the calculus grades while the unaided miss was a guess
graded only by its own confidence.

\paragraph{What the campaign measured, structurally}
Every artifact the loop can admit is meaning-preserving by
construction --- the square of \cref{sec:instrument} \emph{is} the
statement that no new truth is created --- so registry growth never
changes the truth value of any $(p,\varphi)$; it changes only which
truths are reachable within budget. Seven iterations of a discovery
loop therefore produced exactly one demand, and it reads \emph{get a
better engine}. That is not a property of HWMCC: the suite was
chosen native to the hub precisely so no translation debt stood
between the loop and the questions --- which is exactly the
condition under which a translation-producing loop has nothing left
to produce. The campaign measured the closure of an already-closed
set, and found the cost cliff, correctly. The near-term continuation
is scale --- widening the pinned corpus family by family, and the
pre-registered second act, software verification down the full
C-to-solver spine --- but the structural lesson forces a question
the next section takes up: what would it take for the loop to
deposit a \emph{fact} rather than an instrument?
