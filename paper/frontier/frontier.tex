\begin{abstract}
We are constructing a platform for exploring the \emph{frontier of
reducible decidability in practice} --- which questions about
programs can be answered, today, by chains of checked reductions to
mechanized decision procedures within declared budgets --- and,
eventually, for pushing it. This paper describes the platform
designed fresh, after its first campaign, around one organizing
move: \emph{translation and solving are the same kind of edge, and
results are the only currency}. A solver is a pair whose target is
the language of results, so translating is solving; a result is a
replayed witness, a universal claim up to a bound with an optional
certificate, or an honest partial description of how far a route got
and where it failed; and the frontier of a benchmark is exactly the
set of its questions whose best result is not terminal --- no
separate bookkeeping for failure exists. An LLM plays a pinned
benchmark autonomously until a human pulls the plug, conjecturing in
a fixed order that is the vision's core discipline --- new decision
procedures first, then new translations, then new languages:
semantics first, then syntax --- and everything it builds passes one
gate: measured determinism, square or result validity, and two-sided
negative controls. The trust story is one sentence: the LLM never
writes a result; only the kernel does, by running checked code. The
kernel is small, hand-written, and mechanized in Lean~4 --- the
result order is strict, best-per-question ratchets under log growth,
terminal questions stay terminal, grades only climb --- and
everything else is generated content, with the previous generation
of the platform as its first instance, carried over through the gate
it now must pass. We report exactly the experiments the redesign
rests on: the campaign whose structural lesson --- a loop producing
only meaning-preserving artifacts is conservative over truth ---
forced the redesign, and a first demonstration whose three-question
map holds a replayed witness, a bounded claim graded exactly as
strong as its evidence, and an unbounded ask honestly on the
frontier.
\end{abstract}
